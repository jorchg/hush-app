Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026. Hush ("the app") is made by Jorge Garcia Gonzalez.

The short version

Hush does not collect, store, sell or share any of your data. Your recordings, transcripts, summaries and questions never leave your iPhone. We have no servers and no accounts.

What stays on your device

  • Recordings are saved as files on your iPhone, visible in the Files app.
  • Transcription uses Apple's on-device speech recognition. Audio is not sent to Apple or to us.
  • Summaries and Ask use Apple's on-device Foundation Models. Text is not sent anywhere.
  • Face ID is handled by iOS. Hush never sees your biometric data.

What we don't use

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash-reporting services, no third-party SDKs.

Apple services

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store; we receive no payment details. The first time you transcribe in a language, iOS may download Apple's speech model for that language. If you use iCloud Backup, your iPhone's backup (including Hush's files) is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Hush collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page. Questions: rushcentro@gmail.com.
